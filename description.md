# english

A cozy conversion of Heroes III built on VCMI. The mechanics you love are untouched — daily movement, weekly growth, town building, the obelisk hunt — but the world is a friendly countryside of neighborhoods and festivals.

Armies are Friendship Circles. Battles are Encounters where you win over shy creatures with charm and snacks. Castle is now Meadowbrook, home of Ducklings, Fluffy Kittens, and Rainbow Unicorns. The spellbook is a Song & Games Book: Haste is the Skipping Song, Town Portal is the Shortcut Through Grandma's House.

Phase 1 (this version) converts creature, spell, and faction text. Later phases bring new art, sounds, rewritten map objects, and the Grand Festival Grounds endgame.

Nothing scary. Nothing gross. A goose with opinions, at most.
